About DKG Metal Craft Private Limited

DKG Metal Craft Private Limited is a trusted manufacturer and fabricator of commercial kitchen equipment, stainless steel products, and customised metal fabrication solutions. Based in Delhi, we combine skilled craftsmanship, modern manufacturing capabilities, and a commitment to quality to deliver durable and functional solutions for businesses across India.

We specialise in the manufacturing of a wide range of commercial kitchen equipment, including work tables, storage cabinets, counters, bain maries, display counters, sinks, racks, and other customised stainless steel solutions. Every product is designed to meet the practical requirements of commercial kitchens while maintaining high standards of strength, hygiene, durability, and finish.

Our manufacturing capabilities include laser cutting, sheet bending, fabrication, welding, power press work, pipe bending, and rolling, enabling us to provide precision-engineered and customised solutions under one roof. With a focus on quality materials and attention to detail, we work to deliver products that meet our customers’ exact specifications.

At DKG Metal Craft, we believe that every project deserves the right balance of precision, quality, functionality, and value. From standard products to completely customised fabrication, our team works closely with customers to understand their requirements and provide reliable solutions.

Driven by our commitment to customer satisfaction, we aim to build long-term relationships through quality workmanship, dependable service, transparent communication, and on-time delivery.
